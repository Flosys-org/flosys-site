Everything Flo does

Every function, every tool. Tap one to see how it works.

Answers every call, 24/7

Flo picks up on the first ring, any hour, any day, in a warm natural voice with your company name. Your greeting plays all the way through before she listens, so callers never get cut off or hear a robot.

Every call · Nights, weekends, holidays · No hold music

Qualifies the caller

She asks the questions you would: what do they need, where are they, how soon. By the end of the call you know whether it's a real opportunity and what it's worth.

Service, location, timing · Custom questions for your business

Books into your calendar

Connected to your real availability. Flo offers open times, confirms with the caller, and the appointment lands on your calendar before the call ends.

Google Calendar · Real-time availability · No double-booking

Texts booking confirmations

Right after booking, the customer gets a text with the day, time and what to expect. Fewer no-shows, fewer "when was that again?" calls.

Sent to the customer · Your business name on it

Sends appointment reminders

Automatic reminders go out before the appointment so people actually show up. Customers can reply STOP at any time.

Day-before and day-of · Opt-out built in

Texts you a full recap

After every call you get a text with everything that was discussed, any message left, anything Flo wasn't sure about, and a link to the recording.

Every topic · Any mistakes flagged · Link to the call

Records and transcribes every call

Every call, recording and word-for-word transcript live in your dashboard. Search them, replay them, share them with your team.

Searchable · Replayable · Yours

Takes messages

When someone needs you personally, Flo takes a proper message — who, what, how to reach them — and it's in your recap and dashboard immediately.

Name, reason, callback number · Marked urgent when it is

Answers questions about your business

Hours, services, service area, what to expect, how pricing works. Flo knows your business the way a great front-desk hire would after a month on the job.

Trained on your details · Updated any time

Quotes your price ranges

Give Flo your ranges and she'll share them the way you would — honest ballparks that set expectations without boxing you in.

Your numbers · Your rules on what to share

Remembers your callers

Flo keeps a contact book that grows the honest way: she asks who's calling and saves a name only when the caller gives one. She never guesses and never calls someone by the wrong name.

Recognizes repeat callers · Never invents a name

Follows up by text

Missed something, or the caller had to run? Flo can follow up with a text to keep the conversation going and get them booked.

Customer-care updates · Only to people who opted in

Sends you a weekly review

Every week Flo sends a review of what came in: calls answered, appointments booked, messages, and anything that needs your attention.

Calls · Bookings · Messages · What to fix

Works with your number or a new one

Forward your existing line to Flo, or take a new local number. Either way, callers see your business, not ours.

Keep your number · Local numbers available

Web dashboard · Works on your phone
