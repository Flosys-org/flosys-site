Refund & Cancellation Policy

Last updated: November 11, 2026

All sales are final

Every Flosys fee, including any setup fee, is final once paid. Flosys gives no refunds.

Cancel any time

You can cancel at any time by telling your Flosys contact or emailing josh@flosys.org. Your service keeps running until the end of the month you paid for. You are not charged again, and then your line is turned off. On a plan with no setup fee, cancelling in the first 30 days after go-live costs one more month.

Before your line turns off

Turn off call forwarding to your Flo number by your last day, so your calls ring your own phone again. Changed your mind before then? Tell us and your service simply continues.

If we charge you by mistake

If we ever charge you an amount you did not owe, tell us and we will correct it. Correcting our own billing mistake is not a refund of a sale.
