Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 15, 2026

What we collect

When you contact us or become a client, we collect business contact information (name, email, phone, company), the business information you share during intake, and — for services that use your data — records you authorize us to access (for example, customer lists for re-engagement campaigns or job data for your Business Blueprint). Our website may collect basic analytics such as pages visited and general location.

How we use it

To deliver and improve the services you've purchased, to communicate with you about your account, and to operate our business. AI components process your business data solely to produce your deliverables.

What we don't do

We don't sell your information. We don't share your customer lists with other clients or use them for anyone else's campaigns.

Who we share with

Service providers who help us deliver — payment processing (Stripe), email delivery, SMS delivery (Twilio), hosting, and analytics — each only receiving what they need. We may disclose information if the law requires it.

Text messaging (SMS)

If you opt in to FLOSYS SMS Notifications, we collect your mobile phone number, the messages you send us (including opt-in and opt-out keywords), and a record of when and how you consented. We use this only to send the texts you agreed to — missed-call follow-up, appointment reminders, booking confirmations, and customer-care updates — and to answer your replies.

Mobile information will not be shared with third parties or affiliates for their marketing or promotional purposes. Text-messaging opt-in data and consent are never sold, rented, or shared with any third party, except the service providers that deliver the messages (such as our messaging provider, Twilio) or when required by law.

Message frequency varies (up to several messages per week). Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to unsubscribe at any time; reply HELP for help, or contact us at 346-590-6353 or josh@flosys.org. Consent to receive texts is voluntary and is not required to purchase any FLOSYS service.

Opt in on our SMS opt-in page. Full details: SMS Privacy Policy · SMS Terms & Conditions.

Calendar connections (Google, Microsoft and Apple)

Flo books appointments on the Flosys calendar in your dashboard. If you choose to connect a calendar you already use — Google Calendar, Outlook / Microsoft 365, or Apple iCloud — this is what we access and why. Connecting is optional and you can disconnect at any time.

What we access. (1) The list of calendars on your account, so you can choose which ones count as busy time. (2) The events on those calendars, read-only, so Flo knows when you are busy and never double-books you. By default we use only the start and end times; we read an event's title or location only if you turn on “Let Flo look for jobs on my calendar”. (3) One calendar that we create in your account, named “Flo”, where we add, update and remove the appointments Flo books. We cannot edit or delete events on any calendar you created. We never access your email, contacts or files.

How we use it. Only to show your schedule in your Flosys dashboard, to work out the times Flo can offer callers, and to put Flo's bookings on your own calendar. The AI systems that run Flo are given your open and busy times so she can offer appointments. We do not use calendar data for advertising, we do not sell it, and we do not use it to develop, improve or train generalized AI or machine-learning models.

How we store and share it. The sign-in tokens that keep the connection working are encrypted and stored on our servers; we never see or store your Google or Microsoft password. Calendar data is kept in our database with access controls and is shared only with the infrastructure providers that host our service, acting on our behalf, or when the law requires it. No person at Flosys reads your calendar data unless you ask us for support, it is needed for security or to comply with the law.

Keeping and deleting it. We keep calendar data only while the calendar is connected. Disconnect in your dashboard under Calendar › Settings, and we stop syncing, revoke our access with the provider, and delete the busy times we copied. You can also remove Flosys from your Google Account (myaccount.google.com/permissions) or Microsoft account at any time, or email josh@flosys.org to have your calendar data deleted. When your service ends, it is deleted with the rest of your account.

Google user data. Flosys's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Your customers' data

When you give us contact lists or customer records, you remain the owner of that data; we process it on your instructions to deliver your services, and we delete or return it when the engagement ends, on request.

Retention & security

We keep information as long as needed to serve you and meet legal obligations, and we protect it with industry-standard safeguards including encrypted transport and access controls.

Your choices

Email josh@flosys.org to access, correct, or delete your information, or to opt out of marketing messages. Text-message recipients can opt out any time by replying STOP, or get help by replying HELP.
