About FLOSYS

We build the front desk every business wishes it could afford.

FLOSYS is a Texas AI company. Our product is Flo, an AI receptionist that answers every call for your business, books the appointment, and reports back. We started it because we watched good businesses lose customers to missed calls and slow follow-up, and we knew the technology to fix it had arrived.

The team

Three people. One phone line that always gets answered.

How we work

The rules Flo lives by.

Never guess a name.

Flo only calls someone by name if they told her. She'd rather ask than get it wrong in front of your customer.

Nothing untested touches a client.

Every new feature runs on our own practice lines first. Your customers never hear an experiment.

You see everything.

Every call, recording, and transcript is in your dashboard. If Flo makes a mistake, the recap says so.

Flat pricing.

One monthly price. No per-minute meters, no surprise overages.
